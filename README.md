# 📊 Insurance Analytics Executive Dashboard

An end-to-end Business Intelligence solution designed to turn complex operational insurance data into clear, actionable insights across the entire insurance lifecycle.

---

## 🚀 Project Overview
This comprehensive multi-page Power BI dashboard monitors core business metrics across **100K customers**, **150K policies**, and **₹1B+ in total premium**.

---

## 📈 Key Dashboard Pages & Highlights

### 1. Executive Overview
* Tracked core metrics at a glance: **100K Customers**, **150K Policies**, **5K Agents**, **75K Claims**, **20K Renewals**, and **₹1B+ Total Premium**.
* Evaluated premium collections and claim trends across multiple years and quarters.

### 2. Premium & Policies
* Analyzed policy performance across types (Health, Commercial, Motor, Term Life, Whole Life, Home, Travel).
* Tracked **₹972M** in premium collections, **₹73M** pending premium, and active vs. expired policy statuses.

### 3. Claim & Fraud Analytics
* Monitored **75K claims** totaling **₹23B** in claim amounts.
* Evaluated a **64.22% settlement rate**, average processing time, loss ratios (21.53%), and fraud detection metrics (suspected vs. confirmed fraud types).

### 4. Underwriting & Risk
* Assessed an **83.43% approval rate**, pending rates, and declined rates.
* Analyzed customer risk segmentation (Low, Medium, High risk) and risk scores to optimize underwriting performance.

### 5. Renewal & Customers
* Evaluated customer retention, active portfolios (**65K active customers**), lapse rates (7.62%), and customer segmentation (Loyal, Established, New).

### 6. Agent & Channel Performance
* Measured sales productivity, commission payouts (**₹65M+**), and channel contributions across Individual Agents, Digital Partners, Brokers, and Corporate Partners.
